The Content Strategy

A content direction designed to make Group AMANA's engineering expertise more visible, its people more relatable and its capabilities easier for decision-makers to understand.

Pillar 01 · Construct / Manufacture

Blueprint to Built

Make modular construction tangible. Turn DuBox's offsite manufacturing capabilities into short, visually compelling stories that demonstrate how precision manufacturing can transform project delivery timelines.

  • 15–30 second Instagram Reels and LinkedIn videos.
  • Factory-to-site transitions showing modular units being manufactured, transported and installed.
  • Time-lapse sequences and split-screen comparisons between traditional and modular workflows.
  • Engineer-led voiceovers explaining the process in accessible language.
Sample Reel Hook:
"What if months of construction could happen before your building even reaches the site?"

Strategic objective: Build awareness of modular construction and position DuBox as a compelling delivery solution.

Execution & Operational Excellence

30–60–90 Day Brand Ops Roadmap.

My approach is to learn the brand before amplifying it, establish consistent systems before scaling output, and use performance data to continuously improve.

  • Audit existing LinkedIn and Instagram content.
  • Understand brand guidelines, tone of voice and approval workflows.
  • Familiarize myself with Construct, Manufacture and Enhance.
  • Coordinate with project, HSE, HR and technical stakeholders.
  • Build a structured monthly content calendar.
  • Identify content gaps and priority storytelling opportunities.
  • Launch the Blueprint to Built video series.
  • Develop employee spotlights and HSE storytelling.
  • Coordinate photo and video shoots with site teams.
  • Support the production of EPC Decoded PDF carousels.
  • Manage agency briefs, feedback and content approvals.
  • Maintain a consistent publishing and reporting rhythm.
  • Analyze reach, engagement, saves, shares and video retention.
  • Identify the strongest formats and storytelling themes.
  • Optimize content calendars using performance insights.
  • Develop recurring campaign recommendations.
  • Introduce streamlined reporting dashboards.
  • Present a data-informed next-quarter content plan.
My operating principle: Every piece of content should have an objective, an owner, an approval path, a publishing deadline and a measurable outcome. Creativity works best when supported by operational discipline.
